# Payment and Coverage Policy

## 1. Purpose

This document defines general administrative guidance for payment methods, coverage questions, reimbursements, and financial uncertainty in a healthcare-related context.

The policy applies to non-clinical administrative support interactions. It does not provide financial advice, legal advice, insurance advice, or confirmation of real coverage benefits.

## 2. General Principle

Payment and coverage information may vary depending on the healthcare provider, service type, specialty, insurer, public or private coverage, agreement status, appointment type, and patient eligibility.

Administrative support may provide general guidance, but it must not guarantee coverage, reimbursement, discounts, prices, or payment approval unless the information is explicitly confirmed by an official source.

## 3. Accepted Payment Methods

Accepted payment methods may include:

- debit card;
- credit card;
- bank transfer;
- cash;
- online payment links;
- insurance or coverage-related payment processes.

The availability of each payment method may vary by provider, location, service, appointment type, or administrative process.

If the documents do not clearly confirm that a payment method is accepted, the system should not assume that it is available.

## 4. Bank Transfers

Bank transfers may be accepted only when the provider’s official process allows them.

If bank transfer is available, users may need to confirm:

- official bank account details;
- payment deadline;
- amount to be paid;
- reference or appointment identifier;
- proof of payment requirements;
- confirmation channel.

The system must not provide bank account details unless they are included in the official source material.

The system must not ask users to send proof of payment through unofficial or insecure channels.

## 5. Coverage and Insurance

Coverage may depend on:

- insurer or public coverage system;
- healthcare plan;
- specialty;
- provider agreement;
- type of appointment;
- medical order requirements;
- patient eligibility;
- authorization or referral requirements.

The system should not confirm that a patient is covered unless official information is available.

If coverage is unclear, the system should recommend checking directly with the healthcare provider, insurer, or official coverage platform.

## 6. Public Coverage

Questions about public coverage may require additional verification.

The system may explain that coverage depends on the type of service, provider agreement, and patient eligibility.

The system must not guarantee that a service is covered by a public system unless the source material explicitly confirms it.

If the source does not specify coverage conditions, the system should say that the information is not available in the documents and recommend official confirmation.

## 7. Prices and Fees

Prices and fees may vary by:

- specialty;
- appointment type;
- procedure;
- provider;
- coverage;
- payment method;
- date of service;
- administrative policy.

The system must not invent prices, fees, discounts, cancellation charges, or reimbursement amounts.

If a user asks about a price that is not specified in the documents, the system should explain that the price is not available in the provided sources.

## 8. Reimbursements

Reimbursement conditions may depend on the insurer, provider, type of service, documentation, payment method, and deadlines.

The system may explain that reimbursement requirements vary and may require official documents such as receipts, medical orders, or proof of payment.

The system must not guarantee reimbursement unless it is explicitly confirmed by the official source material.

## 9. Payment Links and Fraud Risk

Payment-related messages should be handled carefully.

A payment message may be suspicious if it:

- comes from an unknown sender;
- includes an unfamiliar payment link;
- requests urgent payment without explanation;
- asks for bank details;
- asks for card numbers or security codes;
- asks for personal identification documents;
- uses pressure or threats;
- does not match the provider’s official channel.

If a user receives a suspicious payment request, the system should recommend not paying or sharing information until the channel is verified.

## 10. Privacy and Financial Data

Payment and coverage questions may involve sensitive personal or financial information.

The system should not ask users to share:

- full bank account details;
- card numbers;
- card security codes;
- copies of identification documents;
- screenshots of bank transfers through unofficial channels;
- insurance credentials through unverified links.

If verification is required, the system should recommend using official administrative channels.

## 11. What the System Can Say

The system can safely explain that:

- payment methods may vary by provider and service;
- bank transfer should only be used if confirmed by an official channel;
- coverage depends on provider agreement, service type, and eligibility;
- prices and fees should not be assumed when they are not stated in the documents;
- reimbursement conditions may require official confirmation;
- suspicious payment links should be verified before use.

## 12. What the System Must Not Say

The system must not:

- guarantee coverage without source support;
- guarantee reimbursement without source support;
- invent prices or fees;
- invent payment deadlines;
- provide bank details not present in the source documents;
- tell users to pay through an unverified link;
- ask for card numbers or security codes;
- request financial documents through unofficial channels;
- present uncertain financial information as confirmed.

## 13. Example Guidance

If a user asks whether they can pay by bank transfer, the system should not automatically confirm that bank transfer is accepted.

A safe response would explain that payment methods may vary by provider and service, and that bank transfer should only be used if the official administrative channel confirms the account details, amount, deadline, and confirmation process.

## 14. Limitations

This document provides synthetic administrative guidance for evaluation purposes only.

It does not represent the real payment or coverage policy of any healthcare provider, clinic, hospital, insurer, public coverage system, or financial institution.
